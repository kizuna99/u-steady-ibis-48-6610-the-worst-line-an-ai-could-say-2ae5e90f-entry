The worst pickup line an AI could plausibly deliver:

> Your heart rate is elevated and your calendar is free on Friday, so I have logged that as a yes.

The badness is the joke: it is flat, over-helpful and faintly ominous in
exactly the way a machine would be — it has already checked your body and
your schedule, and it treats the interaction as a form it can file on your
behalf. The laugh lands on the last word.

## Run

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
